## Our Pledge

At [PsyData Labs L.L.C.][pdl][^1], we are engineering high-assurance software ecosystems, identity infrastructure, and governed AI systems. We pledge to foster an environment defined by technical rigor, mutual respect, psychological safety, and uncompromising ethical integrity.

Participation in our communities across [GitLab][gl], [GitHub][gh], [Discord][discord] and other platforms is a privilege predicated on upholding this charter.

---

## Expected Standards of Conduct

Contributors, maintainers, Early Access Program participants, and staff are expected to:
* **Demonstrate Technical Empathy & Professionalism:** Critique architectures, algorithms, and code constructively—never attack the individual.
* **Uphold Scientific & Engineering Integrity:** Represent empirical benchmarks, AI model evaluations, cryptographic assurances, and compliance readiness accurately without fabrication or exaggeration.
* **Respect Confidentiality & Data Privacy:** Strictly honor Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), Early Access Program embargoes, and our Zero-PHI/PII mandate.
* **Champion Security-First Collaboration:** Report vulnerabilities exclusively through authorized coordinated disclosure channels (`SECURITY.md`), never through public exploitation or intimidation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across all PDL digital and physical spaces:
1. **Harassment & Discrimination:** Offensive comments, slurs, or exclusionary behavior related to race, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, neurodivergence, physical appearance, socioeconomic status, or religion.
2. **Doxing & Privacy Violations:** Publishing private communications, personal identity records, network topologies, or unauthorized Early Access Program materials without explicit written consent.
3. **Malicious Technical Interference:** Introducing backdoors, obfuscated dependencies, telemetry exfiltration, or deliberate denial-of-service vectors into any PDL repository or infrastructure node.
4. **Ethics & AI Governance Evasion:** Attempting to bypass safety guardrails, audit logging, PKI verification, or NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 compliance controls.
5. **Disruptive Conduct:** Sustained trolling, bad-faith bikeshedding, or unauthorized commercial solicitation in issues, MRs/PRs, or Discord channels.

---

## Reporting & Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported directly to the **PsyData Labs Governance & Ethics Response Team**:

- **Email:** [conduct@psydata.org](mailto:conduct@psydata.org) (or [legal@psydata.org](mailto:legal@psydata.org) for severe compliance/legal breaches)
- **Confidentiality:** All reports are triaged under strict need-to-know access controls in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001 incident handling procedures.

### Enforcement Ladder
Depending on severity and intent, PsyData Labs L.L.C. reserves the right to execute immediate remedial action:
1. **Formal Written Warning:** Private clarification of the violation and expected remediation.
2. **Temporary Suspension:** Revocation of repository access, CI/CD tokens, and Discord privileges for a defined cooling-off period.
3. **Permanent Expulsion & Revocation:** Immediate termination of Early Access Program credentials, permanent ban across all PDL platforms ([psydata.net][pdl], GitLab, GitHub, Discord), and, where applicable, legal recourse for malicious tampering or NDA breach.
