## Why Support PDL?

Every dollar contributed directly powers our bare-metal infrastructure, confidential compute environments, and compliance certifications (**SOC 2 Type II**, **ISO**/**IEC 27001**, **ISO**/**IEC 42001**, **GDPR** & **UK GDPR**, **HIPAA Safeguards**, and **NIST AI RMF**). **Priority Access** \
Contributors receive earliest admittance to private test environments, staging clusters, pre-release native software builds, and our upcoming decentralized streaming, data verification, and identity infrastructure before any public rollout.

**Developer & SDK Previews** \
Get early access to our Rust crates, Go microservices, TypeScript SDKs, Python pipelines, C/C++/C# bindings, and cryptographic libraries before they are published to public registries. Help shape API design and wire protocol specifications directly with our engineering team.

**Direct Architectural Briefings** \
Participate in private quarterly developer roundtables, roadmap feedback sessions, roadmap voting sessions, RFC reviews, and direct discussions with PDL founders and lead engineers.

**Amplified Commercial Value** \
Rather than treating backing as a sunk cost, mid-to-high EAP tiers act as **amplified pre-purchases** returning **100% to 375% of your contribution** in post-launch platform and compute credits, alongside permanent discount overrides and shareable promotion codes.

**Founding Recognition & Cryptographic Hardware** \
Receive a permanent, cryptographically signed **Founding Supporter** credential bound to your PDL identity, Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes, public or pseudonymous recognition in our official repository acknowledgments, and custom laser-etched FIDO2 hardware security keys.

## Program Onboarding & Wave Topology

To maintain strict operational security and cluster stability, EAP admittance is governed by a five-tier wave topology (**Wave 0** through **Wave 4**):

### Wave 0 - Genesis

Codenamed **Genesis**, our internal staging wave cohort targeting Kernel Vanguards, Apex Individuals, Sovereign, and Institutional Partners. \
**Providing** unthrottled day-zero access to bleeding-edge staging shards (`QUANTA`, `SHARD-A`, `SHARD-B`), experimental feature flags, and raw CI/CD artifact mirrors. All with a private engineer-to-engineer bridge and direct founder access.

### Wave 1 - Day-Zero Alpha

Codenamed **Day-Zero Alpha**, our first official wave targeting Crate Developers, Protocol Architects, Core Builders, Startup, and Growth Partners. \
**Providing** immediate provisioning when internal services reach feature completion, including scoped read tokens for private **Cargo** (**Rust**), **NPM** (**TS**), and **Docker**/**OCI** registries, alongside quarterly architectural briefings and private [Discord][discord] channel access (`#eap-architects`).

### Wave 2 - Closed Alpha

Codenamed **Closed Alpha**, our second wave targets Cipher Enthusiasts, Node Insiders, Pulse Backers, and Stream Runners. \
**Providing** admittance to stabilized alpha sandboxes and pre-release native desktop/CLI binary builds across multiple operating systems with access to the Alpha Issue Tracker and private [Discord][discord] channel access (`#eap-builders`).

### Wave 3 - Early Beta

Codenamed **Early Beta**, for Packet Backers, Signal Supporters, and Pulse Contributors. \
**We provide** prioritized entry to hardened beta services and web portals ahead of general public availability with frequent community polls alongside private [Discord][discord] channel access (`#eap-lounge`).

### Wave 4 - Public Insider (RC)

Codenamed **Public Insider** ("**RC**"), aligning with our free Public Insider Waitlist and Byte Tippers. \
**We provide** phased admittance to public Release Candidate (RC) builds for load and stress testing prior to a `v1.0.0` launch with access to community [Discord][discord] channels and email dispatches.

## Rewards: Credits, Discounts, Identity & Hardware

### Credit Vault & Multipliers

Eligible tiers automatically accumulate **PDL Platform Credits** in an encrypted ledger tied to your identity. Upon commercial launch, credits apply *dollar-for-dollar* toward any PDL billable resource (SYNSECTRIX active identity seats, TELISPECT API lookups, SATP relay bandwidth, or managed compliance hosting):

- **1.0x Base Match (100%)**: Every $1 contributed grants **$1.00** in post-launch platform credits.
- **Amplified Matches (1.10x to 3.75x)**: Higher tiers multiply your capital—up to **$3.75 in commercial credit for every $1 contributed**.
- **No Expiration During Pre-Launch**: Credits never expire while PDL is in pre-launch and remain valid for **36 months** after General Availability[^2].

[^2]: For those who cannot use their credits within a 36-month period or anticipate spending them directly over a longer period of time, we encourage you to reach out to the Early Access Team ([early@psydata.org](mailto:early@psydata.org)) or Billing Team ([billing@psydata.org](mailto:billing@psydata.org)) for an extension or customized arrangement.

### Promo & Discount Code System

Backers receive signed promotional vouchers (`PDL-PROMO-...`) that stack with or apply after platform credits:

- **Personal Plan Discounts** (**5%** to **75% off**): Applies to your personal or developer subscriptions for 1–12 months, multi-year terms, or **for life**.
- **Shareable Referral** / **Client Codes**: Single-use promo codes (**15%** to **50% off**) that you can gift to friends, colleagues, or your own consulting clients.
- **Enterprise MSA Rate Locks** (**15%** to **45% off**): Multi-year or lifetime contractual rate reductions across seat licensing, API overages, and dedicated node leases.

### Digital Identity & Cryptographic Attestations

- **Founding Supporter X.509 / Ed25519 Badge**: A verifiable cryptographic credential permanently bound to your SYNSECTRIX / PDL profile.
- **Apple Wallet** & **Google Wallet Pass**: A cryptographic corporate / supporter pass (`.pkpass` and Google Wallet JWT) displaying your Founding Tier, Member Serial Number, and QR verification payload.
- **Source-Tree** & **Acknowledgments Attribution**: Permanent listing in [docs/ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md](/docs/ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md) across all official PDL repositories (using your legal name, handle, company name, or anonymous pseudonym). Top tiers are hardcoded into the `GENESIS_CREDITS` constant of `v1.0.0` binaries.

### Freebies & Hardware Packs

Physical rewards are shipped worldwide at zero additional shipping cost once your tier threshold or recurring milestone is reached:

- **Sticker & NFC Foil Pack**: Die-cut holographic PDL & Zero-Trust vinyl stickers + a matte-black NFC-enabled metallic Founding Supporter card.
- **Tactical Developer Apparel**: Heavyweight embroidered PDL Developer T-Shirt + custom topographic desk mat, alongside the items in the *Sticker & NFC Foil Pack*.
- **Full Builder Wardrobe**: Heavyweight embroidered PDL tactical hoodie, developer t-shirt, and desk mat, alongside the items in the *Sticker & NFC Foil Pack*.
- **PDL-Provisioned FIDO2 Hardware Key**: Custom laser-etched **YubiKey 5C NFC** (or FIPS-series equivalent) pre-provisioned with a PDL Root CA Founding Attestation Certificate, alongside items from the *Full Builder Wardrobe*.
- **Genesis Executive Vault Box**: CNC-milled serialized anodized aluminum Genesis Monolith plaque, **2x** to **5x custom FIDO2 Hardware Keys**, *Full Builder Wardrobe*, and a signed Founder's letter sealed with a cryptographic verification hash.
