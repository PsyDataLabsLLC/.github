[PsyData Labs L.L.C.][pdl][^1] is an emerging applied systems company and hybrid intelliegence lab. We are engineering foundational technology across five domains:

1. Software Native Ecosystems
2. Technology & Infrastructure
3. Identity, Access Management, and Public Key Infrastructure
4. Compliance and AI Governance
5. Secure Data platforms, infrastructure, and services.

Our core platforms and services are in active development and **coming soon**, we are in the process of launching a *Early Access Program* to allow individuals and organizations to assist with our startup in exchange for early access to our platforms alongside other benefits!

## Strict Compliance, **Day One**

![Zero Trust](https://img.shields.io/badge/Zero%20Trust-Architecture%20Enforced-teal?style=for-the-badge)
![Security First](https://img.shields.io/badge/Security-First-B60205?style=for-the-badge)
![Compliance](https://img.shields.io/badge/Compliance-Global%20Framework%20Aligned-blue?style=for-the-badge)
![Privacy Engineering](https://img.shields.io/badge/Privacy-Engineered%20Into%20Every%20Layer-0E8A16?style=for-the-badge)

We are engineering our infrastructure to strictly adhere to and implement six premier global security, privacy, and artificial intelligence governance standards, such as:

- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO / IEC 27001
- ISO / IEC 42001
- GDPR & UK GDPR
- HIPPA Safeguards
- NIST AI RMF

with more to come, we plan to provide a secure, compliant, auditable, and transparent platform for businesses and organizations of all sizes and individuals.
